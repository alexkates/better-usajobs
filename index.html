The process

Federal hiring, start to finish.

It's structured on purpose — every applicant gets the same fair shot. Here's the whole path, with no surprises.

  1. 1

    Create a profile

    Once. Then it carries your resumes, documents and saved searches to every agency on the site.

  2. 2

    Search for jobs

    Filter by location, salary, schedule, agency or hiring path. Signed in, results get sharper.

  3. 3

    Read the announcement

    All of it. Qualifications are literal here — the requirements listed are the requirements scored.

  4. 4

    Prepare your application

    A guided five-step flow. Progress saves automatically; edit or delete anything before you send it.

  5. 5

    Submit to the agency

    You'll finish in the agency's own system — sometimes a questionnaire, sometimes extra documents.

  6. 6

    Agency review, interview, offer

    Applications are read after the announcement closes and sorted into quality categories. The top category goes to the hiring official, who schedules interviews and selects.
